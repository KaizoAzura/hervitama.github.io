Flowmeter

A flowmeter measures how much liquid, gas, or steam passes through a pipe. The right type depends on the fluid and the process, and correct installation and setting is what keeps the readings accurate.

Magnetic

Measures conductive liquids using electromagnetic induction. No moving parts, well suited to water, wastewater, and slurries.

Faraday's law

Ultrasonic

Uses ultrasonic signals to measure flow velocity, with options that attach outside the pipe without interrupting the process.

Transit-time / Doppler

Coriolis

Measures mass flow directly through vibrating tubes, and can also report density. Used where high accuracy matters.

Coriolis effect

Vortex

Measures flow from the vortices shed behind a bluff body. Commonly used for steam, gases, and liquids.

Vortex shedding

Level Meter

A level meter measures the level of liquids or solids in tanks and process vessels. The appropriate technology depends on the material, operating conditions, and required measurement accuracy.

Radar

Uses microwave signals to measure level without direct contact with the material. Suitable for liquids and solids in demanding process conditions. Converts temperature sensor signals into standardized electrical outputs for monitoring and process control systems.

Microwave Radar

Ultrasonic

Measures level using reflected sound waves. Commonly used for liquid storage tanks and applications where non-contact measurement is preferred.

Ultrasonic Echo

Differential Pressure (DP)

Determines liquid level from the pressure difference caused by the liquid column. Widely used in industrial tanks and process vessels.

Hydrostatic Pressure

Resistance

Detects liquid level through changes in electrical resistance. Suitable for applications using conductive probes or resistive level-sensing elements.

Electrical Resistance

Capacitance

Measures level through changes in electrical capacitance as the material level changes. Can be used for liquids and certain solid materials.

Capacitance Principle

Pressure

Pressure instruments monitor and control pressure in industrial processes to help maintain safe operating conditions and reliable system performance. The right instrument depends on the required measurement range, accuracy, and control function.

Pressure Transmitter

Converts pressure measurements into standardized electrical signals for monitoring, recording, and process control systems.

Pressure Measurement

Pressure Switch

Activates or deactivates an electrical circuit when pressure reaches a predefined setpoint. Commonly used for alarms and automatic control.

Setpoint Switching

Pressure Gauge

Displays pressure directly through a mechanical or digital indicator. Commonly used for local pressure monitoring in pipes, tanks, and industrial equipment.

Mechanical / Digital

Temperature

Temperature instruments measure and monitor thermal conditions in industrial processes. They help maintain product quality, process stability, and equipment safety through accurate temperature measurement and control.

Temperature Transmitter

Converts temperature sensor signals into standardized electrical outputs for monitoring and process control systems.

Signal Conversion

Temperature Element

Detects temperature changes using sensing technologies such as RTDs and thermocouples. Suitable for measuring temperature across a wide range of industrial applications.

RTD / Thermocouple

Valve

Industrial valves regulate, control, or isolate the flow of liquids, gases, and steam within process systems. The appropriate valve depends on the application, operating conditions, and required flow-control performance.

Control Valve

Automatically regulates flow, pressure, or temperature based on signals from a control system to maintain the desired process conditions.

Flow Regulation

Isolation Valve

Opens or closes a process line to control fluid passage and isolate equipment for maintenance or operational requirements.

On / Off Control

Actuated Valve

Uses pneumatic, electric, or hydraulic actuation to operate valves remotely or automatically within industrial processes.

Pneumatic / Electric / Hydraulic

Safety Valve

Releases excess pressure when it exceeds a predetermined limit, helping protect equipment and process systems from overpressure conditions.

Overpressure Protection

Liquid Analysis

Liquid analysis instruments measure chemical and physical parameters to assess water quality and monitor industrial or environmental processes. These measurements support process optimization, water treatment, and environmental monitoring.

pH

Measures the acidity or alkalinity of a liquid to support water treatment, chemical processing, and quality control.

Acidity / Alkalinity

COD

Measures chemical oxygen demand, indicating the amount of oxygen required to chemically oxidize substances in a water sample.

Chemical Oxygen Demand

Ammonia

Measures ammonia concentration in water to help monitor wastewater treatment performance and evaluate water quality.

NH₃ / NH₄⁺

Turbidity

Measures water cloudiness caused by suspended particles. Commonly used in drinking water treatment and environmental monitoring.

Light Scattering

Dissolved Oxygen (DO)

Measures the concentration of oxygen dissolved in water, an important parameter for biological treatment and aquatic environments.

Oxygen Concentration

Chlorine

Measures residual chlorine concentration to monitor disinfection effectiveness in water treatment and distribution systems.

Residual Chlorine

pH Measurement

COD Monitoring

Chemical Oxygen Demand

TSS Monitoring

Measures total suspended solids to evaluate the concentration of suspended particles in wastewater.

Total Suspended Solids

Flow Monitoring

Measures wastewater flow rate to support discharge monitoring, process evaluation, and wastewater management.

Wastewater Flow Rate

Data Acquisition System

Collects, processes, and transmits monitoring data to support continuous observation, reporting, and environmental compliance.

Real-Time Data Transmission
