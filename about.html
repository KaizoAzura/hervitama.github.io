Twenty Years of Earning Trust

In 2006, Hervitama began as a trading company in Sidoarjo, supplying electrical and instrumentation products. There were no shortcuts, only a simple belief: do the work properly, and customers will come back.

Trust became our growth. Customers who relied on us for instruments soon needed more: after-sales support, service, and engineering expertise. We answered by building a team of skilled people who could stand behind every product and every job.

Standards gave that trust a structure. We adopted ISO 9001:2015 for quality and ISO 45001:2018 for safety, and in 2019 we committed to calibration under ISO/IEC 17025:2017. In 2021, KAN accreditation confirmed what our customers already knew: our measurements can be relied on.

Today, twenty years on, the principles are the same as on day one: precision in our work, integrity with our customers, and safety for our people.

  • Consistent standards
